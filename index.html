BIO

Artist  ·  Film Maker  ·  Musician  ·  Photographer
Comedy Writer  ·  Radio Show Host  ·  Armature Coder

Casey Fera has been making things since before it was a good idea. His film work includes Destination Wedding (2018) and Coast (2021).

Casey has played in bands since high school, most notably the Santa Barbara County rock band Ze Jinx in 2002. His solo music is available on Bandcamp.

His photography work is released under the moniker Feral Photography.

He also runs Stages Rehearsal Studio in Lompoc, CA — a space for local musicians to practice, create, and connect.
