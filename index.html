Bio
Artist Film Maker Musician Photographer Comedy Writer Radio Host Armature Coder

Casey Fera has been making things since before it was a good idea. His film work includes Destination Wedding (2018) and Coast (2021).

He's played in bands since high school, most notably the Santa Barbara County rock band Ze Jinx in 2002. Solo music lives on Bandcamp.

Photography is released under the moniker Feral Photography.

He builds small tools, hosts The Local Music Hour on radio, and writes comedy.
