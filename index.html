BIO

Casey Fera is a Santa Barbara County–based filmmaker, musician, photographer, comedy writer, radio host, and amateur coder. His creative work spans independent film production, local music advocacy, and experimental sound tools.

Film & Video

Has worked across camera, lighting, grip, sound, and producing roles on projects including Destination Wedding (2018), Coast (2021), Near Human (2019), Tiger Orange (2014), and Past God (2013). Credits are listed on IMDb.

Music

Has played in bands since high school, including the Santa Barbara County rock band Ze Jinx (formed around 2002). Releases solo music on Bandcamp and Spotify.

Radio & Local Scene

Hosts The Local Music Hour, spotlighting local artists from Lompoc and the surrounding Santa Barbara County communities across rock, alternative, rap, and country.

Studios & Business

Runs Grand Illusion Entertainment and Stages Rehearsal Studio in Lompoc, offering rehearsal space for local bands.

Photography & Tools

Shares photography under the name Feral Photography. Builds free tools for creators and players, including Feral TTS text-to-speech, a baccarat training game, a transparent PNG maker, Spice Master, Piano to Clef, and plugins for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.
